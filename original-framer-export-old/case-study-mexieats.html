MexiEats

A Mexican food ordering and delivery app for busy professionals

Download

PDF

Problem

“In today's fast-paced world, busy professionals often struggle to maintain a healthy diet due to their demanding schedules. With long work hours, constant meetings, and the need to balance personal commitments, finding the time to prepare or even order nutritious meals becomes a challenge. Many workers resort to unhealthy fast food options or skip meals entirely, leading to a decline in overall well-being and productivity"

Details

My role: Sole designer

Responsibilities: End-to-end process

Goal

“To design an intuitive food ordering and delivery app specifically for busy workers, leveraging assistive technology to make the process of ordering healthy Mexican meals quick, easy, and efficient”.

Highlights

Highlights

MexiEats app makes it easy for busy professionals to order healthy, authentic Mexican meals quickly and efficiently.

Understand context

The design process started with understanding our target users—busy professionals seeking convenient, healthy meal options. I conducted extensive user research, including surveys and interviews, to gather insights into their needs and pain points. Based on this feedback, developed personas, journey maps, wireframes, and prototypes, which were tested and refined through iterative user testing. I prioritized intuitive design and assistive technologies for a seamless user experience.

RESEARCH

User testing

From the evaluative research, I learned how satisfied participants

were with the prototype. Additionally, i identified the required changes

to improve the experience. User testing revealed that 5 out of 5

participants were excited about new experiences. Here are the main

takeaways:

•

Some participants wanted a way to track purchases/orders

•

Most participants suggested they want to be able to confirm their transaction before seeing the order confirmed message.

•

It would be beneficial to be able to make payments with a debit card .
