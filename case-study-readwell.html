Problem

Problem

“Adult learners often face low confidence, limited access to resources, and motivation challenges when learning to read. Existing platforms are either geared toward children or lack engaging, adaptive experiences tailored to adults".

Details

Details

Details

My role: Sole designer

Responsibilities: End-to-end process

Goal

Goal

Goal

“Our goal is to create a supportive, non-intimidating interface that respects adult learners’ dignity, deliver personalized, adaptive learning paths based on skill assessments, incorporate gamification to boost engagement and motivation, and provide community and tutor support to alleviate feelings of isolation”.

RESEARCH

Understand context

Research was conducted using a mixed-methods approach:

In-depth interviews with adult non-readers and low-literacy learners. Contextual inquiry with adult education facilitators and literacy coaches. Observational testing of early learning interactions (audio-led tasks, symbol recognition, and phonics).

Secondary research on adult literacy, learning psychology, and accessibility standards. Participants ranged from ages 18–65, with varied educational backgrounds and strong representation from learners using mobile devices as their primary digital tool.

RESEARCH

User testing

From the evaluative research, I learned how satisfied participants

were with the prototype. Additionally, identified the required changes

to improve the experience. User testing revealed that 5 out of 5

participants were excited about new experiences. Here are the main

takeaways:

•

Onboarding completion rate: High when audio guidance is introduced within the first 10 seconds.

•

Users Session length: Average of 6–8 minutes per session.

•

Drop-off points: Text-heavy screens and unclear next steps.

“Engagement drivers: Audio feedback and visible progress indicators increase daily return rates”.

What I would do differently

What I would do differently

I would conduct more frequent usability testing with busy professionals to better validate time-saving features, also prototype and test the payment flow earlier to identify and resolve transaction-related friction points.

Lessons

Lessons

I learned that even a small design change can have a huge impact on the user experience. The most important takeaway for me is to always focus on the real needs of the user when coming up with design ideas and solutions

Tradeoffs

Tradeoffs

Balancing innovation with familiar UI patterns, automation with user control, visual richness with accessibility, and feature depth with usability ensured the experience remained efficient, inclusive, and intuitive without increasing the learning curve.
