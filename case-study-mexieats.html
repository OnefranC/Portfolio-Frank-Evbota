Case study · Native app

MexiEats

A Mexican food ordering and delivery app for busy professionals

Download PDF

Problem

“In today's fast-paced world, busy professionals often struggle to maintain a healthy diet due to their demanding schedules. With long work hours, constant meetings, and the need to balance personal commitments, finding the time to prepare or even order nutritious meals becomes a challenge. Many workers resort to unhealthy fast food options or skip meals entirely, leading to a decline in overall well-being and productivity”.

Details

My role: Sole designer

Responsibilities: End-to-end process

Goal

“To design an intuitive food ordering and delivery app specifically for busy workers, leveraging assistive technology to make the process of ordering healthy Mexican meals quick, easy, and efficient”.

Highlights

MexiEats app makes it easy for busy professionals to order healthy, authentic Mexican meals quickly and efficiently.

Research

User testing

From the evaluative research, I learned how satisfied participants were with the prototype. Additionally, I identified the required changes to improve the experience. User testing revealed that 5 out of 5 participants were excited about new experiences. Here are the main takeaways:

  • • Some participants wanted a way to track purchases/orders.
  • • Most participants suggested they want to be able to confirm their transaction before seeing the order confirmed message.
  • • It would be beneficial to be able to make payments with a debit card.

Retrospective

What I would do differently

I would conduct more frequent usability testing with busy professionals to better validate time-saving features, also prototype and test the payment flow earlier to identify and resolve transaction-related friction points.

Lessons

Throughout the design process, I learned that initial concepts are only a starting point. User research, usability testing, and continuous feedback played a critical role in shaping stronger, more inclusive solutions. The process reinforced that iteration is ongoing, and meaningful improvements emerge through repeated testing and refinement.

Tradeoffs

Balancing speed of ordering with rich meal information, automation with user control, and visual appeal with accessibility kept the experience quick, inclusive, and trustworthy.
